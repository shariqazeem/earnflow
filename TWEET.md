## Recording Tips:
- Use a browser at 1280x720 or similar (not full screen — too wide)
- Slow mouse movements, no jittery clicks
- If on Mac: QuickTime screen recording or OBS
- Add text captions in post-production (CapCut is free and fast)
- Or just use the X video upload — raw screen recording is fine for hackathons
